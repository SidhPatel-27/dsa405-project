# dsa405-project
Course Project REPO; DSA 405

What is this Project?
In this project I am trying to see if a sudden, change in playing climate(a "Climate Shock") negatively impacts a national football team's performance at the FIFA World Cup. By joining national average temperatures with historical tournament standings (2014–2026), the analysis tested if natively hot countries outperformed cold-weather countries in extreme heat.  By classifying teams by their native country's average annual temperature, we can measure the actual impact of temperature on tournament performance. If data shows that hot climate teams over performed in 2022 but not in 2018, sports federations could better justify the massive costs of extreme-heat acclimatization camps and better measure ROI. Additionally, sports bettors could adjust their betting lines based on climate alignment rather than just general geographic proximity.

Findings?
The project found a positive correlation(0.207) between Climate Shock and Goal Difference, contradicting the initial hypothesis. Splitting the performance into mechanical metrics revealed that while extreme heat degrades the defensive stamina of cold-weather nations, their offensive ability remains elite. Ultimately, the data showed that European powerhouses consistently overcame massive climate shocks, demonstrating that there could be a confounding variable, like wealth and infrastructure, that completely overrides the physical disadvantages of native climate alignment.
