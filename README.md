# dsa405-project
Course Project REPO; DSA 405

What is this Project?
In this project I am trying to see if national teams from historically warmer native climates (average annual temperature) earn significantly more points per match in extreme-heat tournaments like the Summer 2026 World Cup compared to more mild tournaments like the 2018 World Cup? The 2026 World Cup was played during one of the hottest months in recent history, whereas a World Cup like the one in 2022 was moved to November/December and utilized air conditioned stadiums. By classifying 2022 and 2026 teams by their native country's average annual temperature, we can measure the actual impact of extreme summer heat on tournament performance. If data shows that hot climate teams over performed in 2026 but not in 2022 or 2018, sports federations could better justify the massive costs of extreme-heat acclimatization camps and better measure ROI. Additionally, sports bettors could adjust their betting lines based on climate alignment rather than just general geographic proximity.

Data Sources
1. Source 1 (Climate): Average yearly temperature by country (Wikipedia).
2. Source 2 (World Cup): Historical FIFA World Cup Final Standings (Wikipedia).
