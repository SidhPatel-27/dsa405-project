# dsa405-project
Course Project REPO; DSA 405

What is this Project?
In this project I am trying to see if national teams from historically warmer native climates (average annual temperature) earn significantly more points per match in extreme-heat tournaments like the Summer 2026 World Cup compared to climate-controlled, mild tournaments like the Winter 2022 World Cup? The 2026 World Cup was played during one of the hottest months in recent history, whereas the 2022 World Cup was moved to November/December and utilized air conditioned stadiums. By classifying 2022 and 2026 teams by their native country's average annual temperature, we can measure the actual impact of extreme summer heat on tournament performance. If data shows that hot climate teams overperformed in 2026 but not in 2022, sports federations could better justify the massive costs of extreme-heat acclimatization camps and better measure ROI. Additionally, sports bettors could adjust their betting lines based on climate alignment rather than just general geographic proximity.

The data comes from 2 sources
1. Sports Reference LLC
2. Wikipedia Foundation
